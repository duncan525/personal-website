Original Project

old site

When I joined UPE, I was required to complete a project in about a month. I wanted to make a website, so I did.

I thought it would be fun to use raw HTML and CSS to put it together. I ended up using some JavaScript as well to get things working the way I wanted them to. I took a learn-by-doing approach to the site; the point was less to communicate information and more to demonstrate that I had learned something and could apply it in an interesting way.

2026 Revamp

reload page

The original project's haphazard layout and design philosophy (or lack thereof) of the kept me from updating the site consistently. Furthermore, I did not consider mobile devices with tall, narrow screens until I had already come up with layouts that worked on my laptop at fullscreen. I decided to remedy these issues in November 2026.

I completed the overhaul in about a week this time. I wanted to make something simple, not ugly, and easy for me to update & expand as necessary. I also wanted it to work better on mobile devices. Given my experience with the previous iteration of the project, it was relatively quick for me to write everything from scratch.
